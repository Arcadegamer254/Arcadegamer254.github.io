Hi — I'm arcadegamer254, a self-taught technology hobbyist. I'm not a professional (yet), and that's exactly the point: everything on this page was made by a curious person figuring things out one commit at a time, in public, mistakes included.

My favourite playground is the space where Android meets Linux. I spent months chasing a real desktop inside a phone — Termux, proot, X11, modded Ubuntu builds — because the idea of a whole computer in your pocket genuinely feels like magic worth understanding.

I learn the honest way: fork things I don't fully understand, read the code, break them, fix them, and occasionally graduate from studying other people's projects to starting my own — like my React-Native Linux-OS experiment and the (very in-progress) Aurora.os.

If it has a terminal, a browser or a bootloader, I've probably tried to poke it. This site is my lab notebook.

  • P.01
    Learn by building.Reading teaches, shipping teaches twice.
  • P.02
    Fork, study, remix — and credit.Open source is my classroom; I say thank you in commits.
  • P.03
